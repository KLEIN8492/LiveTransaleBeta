LiveTranslate is installed

The setup page is opening in your browser. Choose the languages and the local AI, then click Install.

When setup has finished, LiveTranslate lives in the menu bar (LT). Use it to open Control, copy the overlay address for vMix or OBS, and quit.

To uninstall: quit LiveTranslate, delete it from Applications, and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/LiveTranslate.
