LiveTranslate

Live speech translation and subtitles for events — transcribed, translated and shown on screens, vMix/OBS and the audience's phones.

  • Runs entirely on this Mac. Nothing is sent to the cloud.
  • After installing, a setup page opens to choose languages and the local AI. It downloads the models once (2–12 GB); after that no internet is needed.
  • Operate it from any device on the network: http://<this Mac>:8180/control

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or later. 16 GB of memory or more is recommended for the local AI.
