## Design Context

### Users
Live-event operators (AV / sound engineers, production crew, venue staff) running LiveTranslate from a laptop or
iPad at FOH or in a booth, often in a dark room, under time pressure, sometimes with the client watching over their
shoulder. Jobs: set up a session in under a minute, confirm the input signal, go live, monitor captions, and adjust
quality/timing for the event without stopping. The product is sold to businesses, so the console is also part of
the impression the client gets.

### Brand Personality
Premium, discreet, effortless — and technical, dense, confident. It should feel like serious broadcast/pro-audio
equipment: nothing flashy, everything deliberate, the technology disappearing behind calm precision.
Emotional goal: the client trusts it at a glance; the operator never hunts for anything.

### Aesthetic Direction
- References: pro audio console software (DiGiCo/Yamaha console software, L-ISA Controller, Dante Controller) mixed
  with the Linear/Vercel dark dashboards — near-black, almost monochrome, crisp type, hairline structure.
- Theme: dark (dark venues), near-black neutrals with a faint cool tint.
- Colour: almost none. Selection and the primary action are expressed in light neutrals, not hues. Colour is reserved
  for state only: live (red), signal (green/amber/red meter), errors.
- Anti-references: "vibe-coded" colourful dashboards, amber/acid accents everywhere, glows, glassmorphism, gradient
  text, side-stripe callouts, playful display fonts, terminal/mono-everything looks.

### Design Principles
1. State is the only colour. If something is coloured, it means live, signal level or a problem.
2. Hierarchy through type, weight and space — not fills, borders or colour.
3. Console legibility: small uppercase legends (scribble-strip style) label data; values are large and calm.
4. Dense but breathable: tight groups, generous separation between groups; nothing decorative.
5. Every control says exactly what it does; advanced settings are one click away, never in the way.
